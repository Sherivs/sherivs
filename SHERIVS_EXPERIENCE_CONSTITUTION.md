# Sherivs Experience Constitution

Status: `PROPOSED`  
Version target: `ACTIVE-V1`

## Laws

1. **Truth before theatre.** A surface may show only verified data, clearly labeled projections, or explicit empty/planned states.
2. **Authorization before presentation.** Navigation, search, commands, AI context, notifications, and realtime data derive from server-enforced policy. Hidden UI is not security.
3. **Tenant context is mandatory.** Every organization operation resolves tenant, actor, resource, action, purpose, and policy.
4. **Platform is not a tenant.** Sherivs internal operations and customer workspaces use separate identities, routes, policies, and audit scopes.
5. **Runtime is not platform ontology.** Scholar can contribute education capabilities but cannot redefine universal roles, shells, or contracts.
6. **Canonical truth is protected.** Derived intelligence cannot silently alter records, policy, permissions, or outcomes.
7. **Trust is visible.** Important claims expose provenance, timestamp, actor, confidence where applicable, approval, and verification.
8. **One system, controlled variation.** Web, mobile, PWA, public, and runtime surfaces share tokens, semantics, contracts, and state vocabulary.
9. **Content earns a route.** Do not publish placeholder marketing pages or fabricated capabilities, customers, metrics, testimonials, logos, pricing, or social accounts.
10. **Information architecture determines layout.** Cards are one tool, not the default container.
11. **Motion communicates state.** Animation corresponds to navigation, causality, progress, arrival, or verification and honors reduced motion.
12. **Access is a release criterion.** WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, focus behavior, text scaling, contrast, semantics, and touch targets are required.
13. **Mobile is composed, not collapsed.** Prioritize tasks, context, and thumb reach rather than reproducing desktop navigation.
14. **Performance is design quality.** LCP, INP, CLS, TTFB, route weight, fonts, images, and animation cost are measured.
15. **Failure is designed.** Loading, empty, partial, stale, offline, unauthorized, forbidden, expired, warning, error, and recovery states are specified.
16. **Branding is controlled.** Organization tokens may express identity but cannot weaken semantic meaning, contrast, focus, or Sherivs usability.
17. **Compatibility is deliberate.** Customer-facing CoreGrid can migrate to Sherivs; database, package, runtime, migration, bundle, and storage identifiers change only through explicit migration plans.
18. **Frozen means frozen.** V2/V3/deferred capabilities may be inventoried and designed as contracts, but are not implemented or marketed as shipped.
19. **Evidence closes work.** Implementation, test execution, observation, verification, security, accessibility, and performance evidence determine status.

## Spatial grammar

Sherivs experiences express:

```text
Reality → Context → Decision → Action → Evidence
```

- Reality uses stable, information-dense foundations.
- Context uses relationships, hierarchy, and restrained depth.
- Decision uses clear comparisons, authority, and risk.
- Action uses explicit verbs, consequences, and confirmation.
- Evidence uses provenance, time, actor, state, and verification.

## Content voice

Calm, exact, accountable, and human. Prefer concrete verbs and current capability. Avoid superlatives, magical AI language, urgency theatre, unexplained acronyms, and claims that exceed evidence.

## Component rule

Every canonical component documents purpose, anatomy, variants, props, states, responsive behavior, accessibility, motion, examples, anti-patterns, and ownership. A new primitive requires a gap in the existing shadcn/Radix/Lucide stack.

## Definition of experience complete

A surface is complete only when it has an owner, route, capability contract, policy action, real data source, all states, keyboard and screen-reader behavior, responsive behavior, performance budget, automated evidence, human visual review, and rollback path.
