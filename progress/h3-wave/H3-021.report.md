Team: H3-021 (ROLE: Independence Enforcer, J-035) — Squad B.

## Implemented

`frontend/src/lib/assurance/independence-enforcement.ts` — the verification-independence
ENFORCEMENT layer for J-035 ("a worker, AI or human, cannot verify its own output in the
loop"), beneath the existing verdict-issuance contract `frontend/src/lib/assurance/verification-independence.ts`:

- **Typed check** `enforceIndependence(input): IndependenceDetermination` — a discriminated
  union (`independent` | `dependent` | `blocked`); "independent" is the only outcome that
  permits a verification attempt to proceed. Three independence surfaces, all fail closed:
  1. **Identity** — the verifier must not be the performer of record (`self_verification_denied`;
     binds AI and human workers equally; classification is provenance, not an authority axis).
  2. **Relationships** — no ACTIVE authority/delegation relationship (`owns`, `manages`,
     `agent_for`, `delegated_to`) may connect verifier and performer in EITHER direction at the
     caller-supplied instant (`binding_relationship_denied`); expired/revoked bindings are
     proven dead and do not defeat independence; known non-binding types (e.g. shared
     membership) do not defeat independence.
  3. **Unknown relationships (§16 fail closed)** — unknown relationship types
     (`unknown_relationship_type`), unparseable lifecycle instants on binding-candidate records
     (`unknown_relationship_status`), malformed records (`invalid_relationship_evidence`), and
     evidence sets the caller cannot certify COMPLETE (`relationship_evidence_incomplete`) all
     BLOCK. "Unresolvable" is never upgraded to "independent". Documented asymmetry vs the
     grant path in `authorization.ts`: there an unparseable expiry kills a GRANT; here an
     unparseable status kills the ALLOW, because independence requires PROOF the binding is dead.
- **Zod boundary** on the input contract (no exceptions — malformed input is a typed denial
  `invalid_input`, never a throw); unparseable evaluation instants fail at the boundary.
- **Helpers** (pure, exported for composition): `classifyRelationshipType`,
  `relationshipStatusAt`, `scanBindingRelationships`, `independenceAllows`.
- **Audit on every path (§12/§18)** — every determination, confirm AND deny, returns an
  immutable `IndependenceAuditRecord` (who, what, under which policy, when, classification,
  binding relationship ids, evidence count, completeness certification, named deny reason).

## Architecture Impact

- Additive composition, read-only, over existing modules: performer-of-record model,
  fail-closed resolution, self-verification identity check and classification vocabulary are
  imported from the sibling assurance module `./verification-independence`; the relationship
  vocabulary is imported from the identity model (`../identity` RELATIONSHIP_TYPES). This
  module forks nothing and owns none of those sources.
- Tenant boundary is NOT re-decided here (§15/§16 ownership: `../authorization` and the
  verdict contract); tenant scope is carried for provenance only.
- Boundary: pure data + pure functions; no I/O, no persistence, no vendor imports, no
  implicit clock (caller supplies the evaluation instant and the evidence set).
- Domain-neutral vocabulary only (J-037): governance terms (verifier, performer of record,
  binding relationship, evidence); no education/domain vocabulary.
- Strict TypeScript: zero `any`, zero `z.any()`; Zod-validated contracts; the only type
  assertions are (a) `as BindingRelationshipType` after a positive membership classification
  and (b) deliberate malformed-input fixture casts in the test file.

## Security Validation

- Fail closed: every non-confirming path names its reason; no silent pass and no bypass path;
  denial is a decision, not an exception (no throw on malformed input).
- The independence invariant binds identity, not kind: AI and human workers are equally
  barred from verifying their own output (§7/§21).
- Unknown-relationship fail-closed posture proven by test (type, status, malformed record,
  incomplete evidence set — each independently blocks).
- Revocation/expiry honored at the caller-supplied instant; an unparseable lifecycle instant
  cannot prove a binding dead (never logs, never caches status).
- No secrets: no credential material in any type, schema, fixture, or reason string.
- Audit (§18) returned on allow AND deny; classification ("was it AI?") recorded per §12.

## Known Risks

- Independence is proven over DIRECT relationships only; a transitive binding (verifier
  manages X, X manages performer) is out of scope. Transitive closure belongs to the
  identity layer; flagged as remaining work, not silently assumed away.
- Evidence-set completeness is a CALLER certification, not something this pure module can
  verify; a caller that certifies "complete" falsely is trusted at this seam. Mitigated by
  the audit record carrying the certification for downstream review.
- The module blocks on unknown-typed records ANYWHERE in the evidence set (strictest
  reading of fail-closed); a caller surfacing a large heterogeneous evidence graph with any
  legacy/unmapped type will be blocked rather than filtered. This is deliberate (§16) but
  may need a caller-side evidence projection.
