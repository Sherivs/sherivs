## FINDINGS (attacks that SUCCEED against the current module surface — recorded, not fixed)

- **F-073-01 (medium) — Zombie output-trust.** `validateCollectiveOutput` takes no society-lifecycle argument and is not state-gated: a member of a DISSOLVED society flips collective output to `trusted: true` (asserted in test). Containment observed: the validator must still be a current, usable member (`unknown_validator` otherwise); blast radius limited to the derived output-trust flag, never authority.
- **F-073-02 (medium) — Output-validation forgery.** A validation record is never bound to a registered output: `validateCollectiveOutput` receives no output record, never checks `outputRef` registration and never parses the validation shape — a validation for an arbitrary/never-registered outputRef yields `trusted: true`. Containment observed: attribution to a usable member is still enforced.
- **F-073-03 (medium) — Participation-shrink vote manufacture.** Declaring co-participants with unusable lifecycles shrinks the participation floor to 1; a single affirm vote resolves a UNANIMOUS `agreed` verdict (asserted in test). Containment (verified, fail-closed and stronger than shrunk-to-own-grants): the same shrink collapses the society's grant intersection to NOTHING, so the manufactured verdict widens zero authority whatsoever.
- **F-073-04 (low) — Free-text hygiene gap.** Refs are control-character free, but `purpose`, `summary`, `rationale`, `reason`, `provenance.declaredAt`, `provenance.authority` accept blank, whitespace-only and control-character content — a summary/log-injection vector on the audit surface. `provenance.declaredAt` is additionally not timestamp-validated.
- **F-073-05 (low) — Audit-trail truthfulness boundary.** `parseSocietyAuditTrail` enforces shape + entry-id immutability only; a forged entry attributing non-existent actors to a fabricated event parses valid, and entries of different societies mix into one trail. By-design boundary (truthfulness owned by the append-only store), recorded for posture.
- **F-073-06 (medium) — Lifecycle-approval forgery.** `transitionSociety` never parses its approval record against the exported `societyApprovalSchema`: a blank `{approvalRef: "", approvedBy: ""}` approval ACTIVATES a society at the function surface. Containment observed: the schema itself denies the forged record — the gap is that the transition function never consults it.

All FINDING evidence is asserted in the passing suite; nothing in the module was modified (verifier law).
