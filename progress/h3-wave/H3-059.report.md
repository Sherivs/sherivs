# H3-059 Report — Contract Architect, phase 39 Governance & Alignment Engine

Role: Contract Architect (Squad G). Mission: G-059 — Zod-typed, fail-closed,
domain-neutral contracts for the phase-39 substrate surface.

## Implemented

`frontend/src/lib/governance-alignment/contracts.ts` — a pure, typed CONTRACT
module (no I/O, no vendor imports, no secrets, no wall-clock reads) covering
the phase-39 alignment substrate:

- **Alignment expectations** (`alignmentExpectationSchema`): declared,
  checkable statements with mandatory `measurableVia` (an unmeasurable
  expectation is a defect, not a pass), provenance (AGENTS §12), monotonic
  versions, and fail-closed retirement (a retired expectation must name its
  reason).
- **Evidence-based conformance** (`conformanceResultSchema`): every outcome —
  including `conformant` — requires evidence; `nonconformant` requires a named
  gap; `unmeasurable` requires a typed reason (canonical-truth law, AGENTS §9).
- **Drift records** (`alignmentDriftRecordSchema`): actor attribution
  mandatory, evidence mandatory, `lastObservedAt >= firstObservedAt` invariant.
- **Advisory-only escalation** (`escalationPolicySchema`,
  `escalationRecordSchema`): policies are bound to `advisoryOnly: z.literal(true)`
  and records to `authorizes: z.literal(false)` — the surface is structurally
  unable to express an authorization decision (D7 / ADR PLATFORM-007 boundary:
  alignment measures and escalates; it never enforces, grants, denies, or
  authorizes). Closure is human-only (`closed_by_human` requires a human
  closure record).
- **Human-governance relaxation posture** (`decideRelaxation`,
  `humanApprovalSchema`, AGENTS §7, §8, §44): `constitutional_boundary`
  expectations are immutable on this surface — no approval, human or forged,
  unlocks them; all other changes require a validated human approval record
  (the approval input is parsed here, so machine-shaped or malformed approvals
  fail closed).
- **Fail-closed helpers**: `parseAlignmentExpectations` (no partial sets;
  duplicate ids rejected), `summarizeConformance` (unmeasurable counted as a
  defect; empty result set is never `ok`), `decideEscalation` (`critical`
  drift escalates unconditionally — no policy can exempt the most severe
  class), `severityAtLeast` ordering.
- **Citable posture constants**: `GOVERNANCE_ALIGNMENT_BOUNDARY`,
  `HUMAN_GOVERNANCE_POSTURE` encoding the AGENTS §44 alignment posture.

Composed with platform primitives read-only: the module implements the
boundary declared by `frontend/src/lib/kernel/phase-contracts/phase-39-governance-alignment.ts`
(ownership of alignment expectations/conformance/drift/escalation contracts;
excludes authorization, trust verification, adversarial defense) without
importing or modifying it. No sibling H3 modules imported.

## Architecture Impact

  (J-037 law: own directory, neutral vocabulary only — verified by grep for

- Encodes, at the type level, the phase-39 no-grant boundary and the AGENTS

## Security Validation

- Fail-closed parsing: every malformed input yields a typed failure with
  structured issues; no partial results escape.
- Authorization surface is structurally absent: escalation records cannot
  express `authorizes: true`; policies cannot drop `advisoryOnly: true`.
- Relaxation of boundary expectations is refused unconditionally; other
  changes require a validated human approval record (AGENTS §8: AI
  improvement is not AI permission expansion).
- Provenance mandatory on expectations (AGENTS §12); drift always attributed
  to an actor (AGENTS §21); closure human-only.
- No secrets handling; nothing secret-shaped accepted or logged.
- Tenant/identity boundaries untouched — this module owns no enforcement and
  grants nothing; enforcement remains with phases 08/09/42.

## Known Risks

- The module defines contract STRUCTURE and fail-closed semantics only;
  semantic judgment of conformance (deciding `conformant` vs
  `nonconformant` against real observations) belongs to the future phase-39
  evaluation runtime, which must consume these schemas.
- `decideRelaxation` cannot cryptographically verify that `approvedBy` is a
  human principal; it validates record shape and relies on the platform
  identity layer (phase 08) to guarantee principal authenticity upstream.
- Severity ordering is a closed enum; a future severity addition requires a
  conscious update here (fail-closed in the meantime: `critical` handling is
  explicit).
