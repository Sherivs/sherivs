## Known Risks

- F01/F02/F03 are the highest-consequence escapes: they let an untrusted subject (or a compromised classifier) self-clear hostile/indeterminate findings and forge human closure, and the summary then reports a clean surface. Downstream consumers that trust `summary.ok` inherit the laundering. No fix is applied here by mission law.
- Findings F04–F07 share a root cause: cross-record coherence (subjectKind origin, issuer identity, verdict severity vs classification severity, outcome vs signal severity, one outcome per input) is not structurally enforced; the surface trusts per-record self-assertions that its own laws say are untrusted.
- F08 is a zod v4 library behavior surfaced through the module; impact was bounded (verified) but the "unknown keys denied" strictness invariant was not absolute. **REMEDIATED 2026-10-08** by the zod 4.6.5 upgrade (denial now fires; pinned by the H3-078-F08 REMEDIATED test).
