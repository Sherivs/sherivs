## Implemented

`tests/unit/h3-097-kg-provenance.test.ts` — an offline vitest assertion suite
(36 tests, zero new dependencies, strict TypeScript, no `any`) proving that the
phase-48 knowledge-graph relationship contract
(`frontend/src/lib/knowledge-graph/contracts.ts`, G-068's module) honors the
§11 Knowledge Graph Principle end-to-end:

1. **Mandatory fields.** Provenance, ownership, authorization and confidence
   are mandatory on every relationship. An edge missing any of the four
   (or all four) fails `parseGraphRelationship` with `malformed_edge`.
2. **Provenance depth (§12).** Provenance answers "where did this come from":
   createdBy (non-blank), originKind, originRef, createdAt, the mandatory
   `aiGenerated` declaration, and bounded transformation history (33 entries
   rejected). Unknown provenance fields are rejected (strict shape).
3. **Canonical truth (§9).** An inferred edge must cite `inferenceMethodRef`;
   an asserted edge must not carry one; a declared AI-generated edge parses.
4. **Ownership.** An accountable, non-blank `ownerRef` is mandatory; unknown
   ownership fields are rejected.
5. **Authorization (§13).** `visibility` and `sensitivity` are mandatory;
   federated edges require `federationRef` AND `approvalRef`; a
   `federationRef` on a tenant-scoped edge is rejected.
6. **Confidence.** Tier must match its numeric band; high/confirmed edges must
   cite evidence; publication refuses edges below
   `GRAPH_CONFIDENCE_PUBLISH_FLOOR` (`confidence_below_floor`).
7. **No consumer leak.** Edges missing any of the four fields are refused by
   `publishRelationship` (`edge_malformed`) and excluded from
   `evaluateTraversal` as `malformed_edge` (counted, never absorbed; the far
   endpoint becomes unreachable). A fully governed edge traverses with all
   four §11 fields attached and intact on the projected `SubgraphEdge`.
8. **Posture.** The module declares
   `relationshipPolicy = "provenance-ownership-authorization-confidence-mandatory"`,
   `defaultDecision = "deny"`, and query-time-filtered gated traversal.
