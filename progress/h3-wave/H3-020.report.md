## Implemented

`frontend/src/lib/approval/chains.ts` — the ESCALATE / REQUIRE_APPROVAL resolution-flow contracts (Constitution §7, §12, §16, §21, §29). The §16 authorization vocabulary has five outcomes; ALLOW/DENY/REDACT are terminal in the engine, and this module gives the remaining two (ESCALATE, REQUIRE_APPROVAL) a wired resolution flow:

1. **Case opening** — `openResolutionFlow()` / `openResolutionFlowForAuthorizationOutcome()` turn a deferred engine outcome into a content-addressed `EscalationCase` (request, tenant, policy, risk tier, requester kind, subject digest, engine provenance). Zod-validated; terminal decisions (ALLOW/DENY/REDACT) fail closed with `decision_not_routable` — this module never re-decides what the engine decided.
2. **Risk-classified routing** — `routeToApprovalChain()` routes a case to a declared human approval chain. `routine → single-resolver (1)`, `elevated → two-person (2)`, `critical → quorum (3)`; the routing pool must satisfy the mandated depth with independent, eligible humans (capability + human-only + requester-excluded). Fail-closed: wrong tenant, invalid deadline, or an insufficient pool yields a typed `RoutingBlocked` — the flow never waits on an impossible chain and never falls back to auto-allow.
3. **Human resolution records** — `recordResolution()` appends evidence-bearing §12 provenance (who, verdict, capability + tenant evidence, when, rationale). Laws enforced at record time: AI identities can never resolve (`resolver_not_human`), the requester can never self-resolve (`self_resolution_rejected`), capability/tenant/pool membership enforced, append-only uniqueness (no duplicate record id, no double-position resolver), case window and deadline enforced, evidence required.
4. **Typed flow outcomes** — `resolveFlow()` produces `approved | rejected | pending | blocked_no_eligible_resolver | blocked_unresolved | invalid`. Records are re-derived against the case and routing at the evaluation instant from their own evidence snapshots, so a forged or drifted record surfaces as `invalid` — never absorbed.
5. **Fail-closed when no approver resolves** — a chain that cannot satisfy the mandated depth blocks at routing time; a chain whose deadline passes without human closure yields `blocked_unresolved` forever. Blocked is never downgraded to approved and never silently dropped.
6. **Execution gate** — `gateExecutionOnResolution()` is the last check before the subject of a deferred decision proceeds: ONLY the `approved` outcome (mandated depth of independent, eligible, evidence-bearing human resolutions) is `ALLOWED`; every other outcome is `DENIED` with named reasons and a full, deterministic, content-addressed audit record (`ResolutionExecutionAudit`). The gate never throws — denial is a recorded decision, not an exception, so no bypass path exists.

Composition (read-only): decision vocabulary types from `frontend/src/lib/identity.ts` (`AuthorizationResult`) validated against the engine's §16 contract; canonical content digests from `frontend/src/lib/ai-gateway/reasoning-provenance.ts` (`digestContent`). The command modules (`frontend/src/lib/command/*`) are store-facing persistence contracts — deliberately not coupled into this pure flow; the durability adapter mission can implement the store behind these contracts unchanged.

## Security Validation

- Fail-closed by construction: unmet depth → blocked/denied, never downgraded; no silent drop; every denial audited.
- AI sovereignty (§7/§21): AI identities cannot occupy resolver positions at routing, record, resolution re-derivation, or gate time; AI requesters are provenance-recorded.
- Independence: requester exclusion at routing + `self_resolution_rejected` + re-derivation at resolution time; one resolver occupies at most one position.
- Tenant boundary (§13): chain scope, resolver evidence, and record re-derivation all enforce the case tenant; mismatch is a typed rejection.
- Provenance (§12): subject digest binding — any subject change yields a different case, voiding prior content bindings; records carry capability + tenant evidence snapshots.
- Forgery resistance: `resolveFlow` re-derives every record from its own evidence snapshot; forged sets surface as `invalid` (tested with 9 forgery shapes).
- Secrets: none printed, logged, or written; no credential-gated code in this module.
