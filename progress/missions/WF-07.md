## Existing work found

- WF-01 to WF-03 already had `wf_approval` (with `wf_approval_not_self`), the `workflows.approve`
  permission, the `workflows.manage` / `workflows.approve` separation-of-duties rule and
  `wf_audit_append`. WF-07 builds on them and does not duplicate any of it.
- The Jarvis owner console (`jarvis-bridge/step-up.ts`, `auth/webauthn.server.ts`) already had a
  passkey step-up pattern (`assertionOptions` / `verifyAssertion` with a caller-supplied challenge).
  WF-07 reuses the WebAuthn service unchanged and adds only its own binding (`approval-step-up.ts`).
- WF-06 (#455, not yet merged) left drafts with no way to be reviewed (ADR-147) and a
  `wf_action_draft_cap_check` that forbade approving a capped draft at all. WF-07 replaces it.
- ADR-115 (referenced by ADR-147) is not in the repository. ADR-122 does not depend on it.

## Assumption (open decision 4)

Who may author and who may approve is undecided. Deciding is gated on the existing
`workflows.approve` permission, `wf_approval_not_self`, the author and last-editor exclusion and the
SoD rule. The admin pass-through that grants view does not grant approve. See ADR-122.

## Round 2 review fixes (26 Nov 2026)

- The row-level approval gate (`wf_approval_decision_gate`) now counts only approving votes whose `content_digest` equals the
  current digest, the same rule `wf_decide_approval` applies. A vote cast on older content can no longer satisfy a direct
  update of a high-risk approval. Harness case added (98 checks); the mutant without the filter is caught.
- The rollback no longer re-grants EXECUTE on the trigger function `wf_action_sandbox_outbox_guard()` to `service_role`
  (a trigger function needs no EXECUTE once the trigger exists). Rollback then re-apply verified on a copy.
- "Partial dispatch can leave a step stuck": on this branch alone the sweep would not list a draft whose executed record
  exists while its step is still waiting. WF-08 section 9 lists such a draft with `needs_dispatch = false` and resumes it
  without dispatching twice, so the stack as a whole closes it. Verified only in WF-08's harness, not here.

## Not verified

- A real authenticator. The e2e stubs the WebAuthn service at its boundary; it signs exactly the
  challenge the production code issued (or a wrong one on purpose). The binding, the verification
  callback, the evidence row and the single-use rule are real. The browser `startAuthentication`
  step and the real signature check with WF-07's challenge have not been run.
- The screen in a browser. There is no component test tooling here; the screen is type-checked,
  linted, covered by static tests and bundle-checked, not rendered. 44px targets are asserted in the
  CSS, not measured. No accessibility audit.
- A hosted Supabase (including PostgREST exposure of the new functions): staging is for the lead.
  PostgreSQL 17 is covered by the CI run above.
- Concurrent decisions by two approvers at once. `wf_decide_approval` locks the approval row, but a
  two-session test was not written.
- The WF-05 e2e locally (it needs the seeded synthetic school from `gen.mjs`); the CI run above passed it.
- Resuming a plain approval-kind workflow step after its approval. Not built; only drafts continue.
- Any notification to approvers. None is sent; `wf_approver_candidates` exists but has no consumer.
- Live delivery of an approved draft: refused in v1, so no live path is exercised.

## Founder decisions needed

1. Who may author and who may approve (open decision 4). WF-07 assumes the existing permission and SoD
   rules and widens nothing.
2. Rejecting needs no passkey (it moves to the safe state). Confirm.
3. A reject or an expiry cancels the whole run. Confirm, or ask for a softer behaviour.
4. Every draft, not only capped ones, now needs an approval before it can proceed.
5. Money movement and safeguarding cannot be removed from the two-person list, and the count cannot
   drop below 2, whatever an organization sets. Confirm.
6. Approval notification (the inbox is pull-based), and whether the Approvals link should be hidden
   from people who cannot approve, which needs a permission signal in the shell.
7. Email, SMS and WhatsApp template bodies are not in the digest (the comms store is not read in SQL
   because of the neutrality guard); WF-06's template check re-runs at dispatch.
8. The ADR provenance id `W-007` follows the WF-01 precedent (`W-001`); the provenance guard has no
   matcher for the `WF-NN` form.
