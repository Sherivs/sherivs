## Implemented

- Added workflow view/manage/run/approve permissions to the existing catalog and kept manager and approver template defaults separate.
- Added the manage/approve SoD catalog rule and a migration preflight for conflicting active assignments.
- Added a durable `updated_by` stamp for workflow drafts and database guards that prevent an author or last editor from publishing or deciding an approval for that version.
- Added the global, versioned `wf_template` catalogue with forced RLS, authenticated read access, service-role write access, and three generic seed records.
- Added focused static and local-database checks, including CI wiring for the disposable simulation database.

## Review (Tumi)

- `wf_version_guard()` now uses `coalesce(auth.uid(), NEW.created_by)` on insert. A signed-in person cannot forge `created_by` or `updated_by`: `wf_version_05_editor_stamp` overwrites both with `auth.uid()`, and `created_by` stays immutable on update. Only the service path (no `auth.uid()`) may supply authorship, and it must supply it or the insert fails closed.
- Separation of duties holds for administrators: publishing and approval decisions are service-path only (authenticated has no publish path and no `UPDATE` on `wf_approval`), and the guards compare people, with no `can_administer_org` or super-admin exemption.
- No new `SECURITY DEFINER` functions. The three new trigger functions are `SECURITY INVOKER`, `search_path = ''`, with no execute grant to `PUBLIC`, `anon`, `authenticated` or `service_role`.
- The rollback recreates `trg_role_template_permissions_guard` exactly as defined in SHERIVS-017 (compared with `pg_get_triggerdef` on production).
- CI fixes requested from Copilot and delivered in `64d0200`: WF-01 concurrency fixture now records author, editor and publisher; ADR-104 provenance rows; prettier. Migration and rollback SQL unchanged by review.
- Follow-up (not a defect): the upgraded blueprint asks the template catalogue to carry agent, autonomy, release, lawful-basis, data-class and eval fields. Issue #430 predates that and `wf_template` does not have them yet; they are carried into the next engine mission.
