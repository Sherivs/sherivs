## Gaps found (each reproduced in `tests/sql/m9-finance-core.sql` before the fix)

1. Payroll: on INSERT the preparer is whatever the client sends (`prepared_by` only has a default), so one person can insert a line "prepared by" a colleague and approve it. Amounts of an approved or paid line can be changed with no check. A line can be approved with no approver when written by a server path.
2. Expenses: on INSERT `created_by` is client-sent, so the raiser can name someone else as raiser and approve their own expense.
3. Payroll and expenses had no audit trigger.
4. Refunds: the decider of a fee refund could be the cashier who recorded the payment; no rule stops one person holding `payments.record_cash` and `payments.refund`. The online Paystack refund path (`cg_financial_reversal`) had no check against cashiers.
5. Reconciliation of cash: no day close or cash reconciliation existed, so "the reconciler is not the cashier for that day" could not be enforced at all.
6. The `accountant` template ships `payments.record_cash` on by default next to the mandatory `payments.reconcile`; the existing SoD rule makes every default accountant assignment fail.
7. Relief (A05) never reached the M07 ledger, so a term with relief could not balance.
