## Offline design

- Attendance: kept on the existing M05 queue. It was not moved to the generic outbox: moving it would not gain anything, and the A07/M05 tests pin its behaviour.
- CA scores: generic outbox, kind `ca_score`, key `ca:<year>:<term>:<subject>:<learner>:<saveId>`.
  - A double tap, a second drain or a late re-enqueue sends nothing more.
  - Later edits send in order.
  - Server refusals are final and shown with the server's reason.
  - Rows queued under another role are refused.
- Attendance corrections and lesson notes need a connection. Corrections carry a client key, so a repeat is a server-side replay.
