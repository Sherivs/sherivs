## Existing work found

- WF-01 to WF-03 already had the run, step and event tables, the guards that make a finished run and a
  dead step immutable, `wf_can`, `workflows.view`, `workflows.run`, `workflows.manage` and
  `wf_audit_append`. WF-08 builds on them and weakens none.
- WF-07 already cancels pending approvals, undecided drafts and waiting steps when a run ends
  (`wf_run_80_close_approvals`), and has the `wf_act_as` / `wf_act_restore` identity binding. WF-08 reuses both.
- The engine keeps its run state in a `runtime.state` event. A retry needs it, and it can hold free text,
  so it is copied by the retry function and excluded from the history.
- No run-history screen, redaction function, retry or cancel existed. `run-history` and `workflow-run` names
  were free. ADR-123 and issue #468 were free; the reserved migration number was not (below).

## What the e2e covers

Org A: a 26-recipient message (capped, two-person, high-risk) is drafted, approved by two people with
passkeys (WebAuthn stubbed at its boundary), and the run is then cancelled before the sweep. The sweep
dispatches nothing, the broker ledger has no decision, the draft stays approved, the run stays cancelled,
cancel is idempotent. Org B: a step fails because its template is missing; history shows it; a viewer
and another organization are refused; the template is fixed; retry makes one new linked run, a repeat
returns the same run, and the real runtime carries it to the sandbox outbox exactly once and to its next
step. History output is checked for recipient ids, addresses, template text and other organizations' ids
(none found). Audit holds exactly one cancel and one retry request.

## Founder decisions needed

1. Migration number: keep `20261117000950` or renumber (the reserved `..001000` is taken on PR #448's branch).
2. `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` inside the migration: keep, or follow the "no transaction" convention if there is one.
3. Raw reads of the WF-01 run, step and event tables by `workflows.view` holders are not redacted and still
   exist as WF-01 defined them. Should those read policies be narrowed so the history functions are the only path?
4. Retry is refused while workflows are disabled for the organization (cancel and history still work). Confirm.
5. Retry refuses any step that would end with more autonomy than it had. Confirm that strictness.
6. Cancel needs `workflows.run` or `workflows.manage`; retry needs `workflows.run`. Confirm, or ask for a
   dedicated permission.
7. Sub-workflow children are not cancelled with their parent, and approval-kind dead steps are never retried.
8. No retention: old runs and events stay until WF-12.
9. The ADR provenance id `W-008` follows the WF-01 and WF-07 precedent; the provenance guard has no matcher
   for the `WF-NN` form.
