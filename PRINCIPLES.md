1. COREGRID ARCHITECTURAL IDENTITY
CoreGrid is not fundamentally:
• an LMS
• an ERP
• a CRM
• an automation platform
• a chatbot
• a marketplace
• a cloud provider
• a collection of dashboards
• a single AI model
• a single autonomous agent
• a single-domain SaaS application
CoreGrid is:
A universal computational infrastructure for representing, understanding, remembering, reasoning about, simulating, creating, operating, governing, and evolving complex realities.
Operationally, CoreGrid may also be understood as an:
AI-native reality and institutional intelligence runtime capable of composing domain-specific computational environments from universal capabilities.
The distinction is important.
CoreGrid is the substrate.
Domain runtimes are instantiations of that substrate.
Applications are compositions operating within those runtimes.
Agents are actors operating through governed capabilities.
Models are replaceable intelligence resources.
