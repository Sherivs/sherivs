# Sherivs Information Architecture

## Public IA

Not: Hero → cards → testimonials → pricing.

Narrative: Brand → category → organizational reality → intelligence kinds → capabilities → AI workforce → mission process → adaptive experiences → Scholar as a runtime → governance → enterprise architecture → category → CTA.

Primary nav: Platform, Solutions, Security, Pricing, Resources.  
Footer: Platform, Solutions, Company, Legal, Status. No invented social.

## Organization public IA

Generic sections: identity, hero, about, programs, people, events, announcements, resources, contact, policies, gallery.  
Scholar-only: admissions, faculty, facilities. Resolved by `resolveOrgPublicExperience`.

## Organization workspace IA

Capability-driven from `scholar-nav-catalog` through `workspace-engine/navigation`. Items appear when capabilities and permissions allow. Do not hardcode a 20-item universal sidebar as always-visible.

Potential universal groups (shown only when capable): Overview, People, Work, Missions, Knowledge, Communications, Calendar, Files, Automation, AI Workforce, Integrations, Analytics, Governance, Settings.

## Internal Sherivs IA

Executive/ops surfaces under super-admin: tenants, workers, missions, governance, trust, communication steward, runtime contracts, finance/academic/scheduling intelligence. Not linked from tenant chrome.

## Account IA

Profile, appearance, accessibility, privacy, password. Honest gap: MFA/SSO/passkeys not enabled.
