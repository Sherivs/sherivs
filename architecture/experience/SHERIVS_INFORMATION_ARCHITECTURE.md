# Sherivs Information Architecture

**Version-target:** ACTIVE-V1

## Public homepage narrative (not a card stack)

01 Brand → 02 Category → 03 Reality → 04 Intelligence → 05 AI workforce → 06 Missions → 07 Adaptive experiences → 08 Domain runtimes → 09 Governance → 10 Enterprise architecture → 11 Category close → 12 CTA  
Plus anchored capability, security, and pricing *pointers* (pricing table lives on `/pricing`).

## Organization workspace (capability-filtered)

Potential items: Overview, Reality, People, Teams, Work, Missions, Knowledge, Memory, Communications, Calendar, Tasks, Files, Data, Reports, Automation, AI Workforce, Integrations, Analytics, Governance, Settings.

Today the installed Scholar runtime contributes role portals. Items the organization cannot use must not appear as decorative navigation.

## Internal Sherivs

Executive, Jarvis, Missions, AI Workforce, Customers, Organizations, Product, Engineering, Architecture, Infrastructure, Security, Operations, Support, Finance, Research, Analytics, Releases, Incidents, Governance, Documentation — behind super-admin / command-centre authorization only.

## Account / organization settings hierarchy

Personal: profile, appearance, accessibility, notifications, security, privacy.  
Organization: general, members, roles, security, brand, integrations, billing, audit — via existing admin settings where those contracts exist.  
Missing settings are omitted, not mocked.
