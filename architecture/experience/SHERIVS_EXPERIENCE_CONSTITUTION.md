# Sherivs Experience Constitution

Status: Governing proposal for ACTIVE-V1 experience work

## Principles

1. **Truth before presentation.** The interface projects canonical state and labels derived,
   inferred, stale, simulated, and unknown information.
2. **Authorization before discovery.** Navigation, search, commands, AI, notifications, and deep
   links never confer permission.
3. **Tenant context everywhere.** Every private experience resolves identity, tenant, resource,
   purpose, policy, and risk.
4. **One platform, contributed runtimes.** The universal shell is platform-owned. Scholar and
   future runtimes contribute through governed registries; they do not redefine the shell.
5. **Capability-driven composition.** Organization type, runtime, capability, role, policy, and
   context determine experience. Fixed role portals are migration debt.
6. **Trust is visible.** Evidence, source, provenance, actor, timestamp, confidence, approval, and
   verification are first-class visual primitives.
7. **AI is not authority.** Observation, inference, recommendation, proposed action, execution,
   evidence, and verification are visually and operationally distinct.
8. **States are complete.** Default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, success, error,
   warning, empty, partial, offline, unauthorized, forbidden, expired, and stale are designed.
9. **Mobile is a distinct composition.** It preserves task priority and context rather than
   shrinking desktop.
10. **Accessibility is structural.** WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard operation, semantic HTML, focus
    management, contrast, text scaling, reduced motion, and touch targets are release gates.
11. **Performance is experience.** LCP, INP, CLS, TTFB, route cost, image/font cost, and bundle
    change require evidence.
12. **Content is governed data.** Every published item has provenance, status, access level,
    locale, canonical URL, and publication history.
13. **Brand is controlled.** Sherivs identity is consistent; organization branding is a bounded
    token layer and cannot reduce usability.
14. **No fabricated proof.** No fake customers, metrics, AI activity, integrations, social
    accounts, testimonials, uptime, or capability claims.
15. **No generic composition.** Cards, gradients, glass, charts, and animation require an
    information or state purpose.
16. **Progress is evidence-based.** Designed, documented, implemented, tested, verified, deployed,
    and operational are separate states.

## Spatial grammar

Sherivs uses four semantic planes:

`Reality → Intelligence → Action → Evidence`

- **Reality:** stable, information-dense source state.
- **Intelligence:** derived interpretation with source and confidence.
- **Action:** available or proposed operations with authority and consequence.
- **Evidence:** immutable or append-only outcome, provenance, and verification.

Depth communicates plane and interaction priority, not decoration. A border groups; elevation
floats; translucency reveals context; a modal interrupts. Do not substitute a card for structure.

## Content voice

Sherivs copy is precise, calm, direct, and accountable. Prefer concrete verbs and current state.
Avoid “magic,” anthropomorphic AI, unverifiable superlatives, urgency theater, and hidden
consequences. Errors explain what happened, what remains safe, and the permitted next step.

## Component law

Each canonical component documents purpose, anatomy, variants, properties, state matrix,
responsive behavior, accessibility, motion, examples, anti-patterns, and data/security assumptions.
A component may not accept unvalidated arbitrary component names or executable behavior from data.

## Review questions

- Is the source of truth apparent?
- Is tenant and workspace context apparent where consequential?
- Can a user distinguish fact, inference, and recommendation?
- Does the available action match actual authority?
- Is the information structure useful without animation, color, or glass?
- Are loading, zero, failure, stale, offline, and denied states honest?
- Does keyboard and screen-reader operation preserve the same outcome?
- Does the composition work with long localized content and real data ranges?
- Could this belong to a generic SaaS template? If yes, revisit the information architecture.

## Prohibitions

Do not ship decorative charts, fake live indicators, giant unsupported statistics, generic AI
orbs, particles, gratuitous parallax, endless card grids, organization color overrides of semantic
states, client-only access controls, or customer links into Sherivs internal routes.

## Version boundary

This constitution governs ACTIVE-V1 experience implementation. It may document V2, V3, and future
surfaces, but those remain frozen until an explicit human authorization record unfreezes them.
