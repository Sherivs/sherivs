# Sherivs Experience Constitution

**Version-target:** ACTIVE-V1  
**Authority:** Experience Architecture program under AGENTS.md and V5.3

## Laws

1. **Brand law.** Customer-facing copy says Sherivs. CoreGrid remains the engineering kernel, package, and schema name.
2. **Category law.** The product is Institutional Intelligence Infrastructure. It is not a school-management UI that grew a marketing site.
3. **Runtime neutrality.** A runtime consumes the platform. Scholar is the first installed runtime, not the product identity. Login does not assume an education portal.
4. **Honesty law.** No fake metrics, customers, logos, social accounts, live AI theatre, or pages without capability.
5. **Spatial law.** Layout follows information architecture (list, table, timeline, panel, canvas, command) — cards are not the default.
6. **Glass law.** Translucency is for chrome and overlays. It is not the brand.
7. **Trust law.** Observation, inference, recommendation, action, evidence, and verification are distinct.
8. **Capability law.** Navigation is capability- and permission-driven. Missing items are omitted or shown as empty/unauthorized — never as decorative tiles.
9. **Accessibility law.** Keyboard, focus, semantics, contrast, reduced motion, skip link. Target WCAG 2.2 AA on new surfaces.
10. **Motion law.** Motion represents state. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses decorative animation.
11. **Tenant law.** Organization branding may tint signal color; it may not destroy contrast, focus, or Sherivs hierarchy.
12. **Security law.** UI is not enforcement. Command, search, and deep links obey the same authorization as pages.

## Visual quality bar

A surface is not complete if it could belong to any generic SaaS, if hierarchy is unclear, if empty/loading/error/unauthorized states are missing, if it only works in one viewport, or if it hardcodes Scholar as the platform.
