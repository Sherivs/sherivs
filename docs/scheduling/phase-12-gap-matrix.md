- `frontend/src/lib/scheduling-constraints.ts` — hard/soft constraint model and evaluators.

- `frontend/src/lib/runtime/scholar/timetable.functions.ts` — current single-class AI draft and legacy persistence path; the solver is not wired into it in S1.

## Version-target and delivery status

**Version target: V1 organizational capability, narrowly authorized under #227.** The broad Phase 12 classification is not changed. The S1 migration is file-only; its tenant boundary uses the neutral membership helper and organization unit, and its solver is an extension of `scheduling-core`, not a second scheduling engine.

- **S0:** documented baseline coverage above.
