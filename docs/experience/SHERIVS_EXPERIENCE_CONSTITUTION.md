# Sherivs Experience Constitution

Version target: **V1**.

This is the rule set for experience work. A screen that looks expensive and violates a rule is incorrect.

## Identity

1. Customer-facing copy says **Sherivs**.
2. Technical identifiers — types, tables, migrations, environment keys, module paths — stay **CoreGrid** unless a human authorizes a migration.
3. An installed runtime is named by the runtime layer. The platform does not become that runtime.
4. Education sections render only when `runtime:scholar` is installed for that context.

## Truth

5. Do not publish a page that needs a fact the repository does not have: customers, headcount, offices, social accounts, uptime percentages, certifications, SDKs, or a blog.
6. Do not draw a chart with invented numbers.
7. Do not animate "thinking" unless a real request is in flight.
8. AI output is labeled. Observation, inference, recommendation, action, evidence, and verification are different states.
9. A worker cannot verify their own mission.

## Authority

10. Navigation, command palettes, and hidden buttons are not authorization.
11. `resolveExperienceConfiguration` and `admitCommands` withhold. They do not grant.
12. Internal routes are not linked from the public shell.
13. Client-supplied tenant ids and roles are not trusted.

## Form

14. The public homepage is a narrative of planes, not a hero, card grid, testimonial row, and pricing table.
15. Cards are a choice for repeated records, not the default layout.
16. Glass is allowed where a layer must sit above another layer. It is not the surface of every block.
17. One signal color. No rainbow gradients. No decorative particles.
18. Type scale, spacing, and motion come from the token contract.

## Inclusion and performance

19. Keyboard, visible focus, skip link, and `prefers-reduced-motion` are required on new surfaces.
20. Contrast pairs in `SHERIVS_CONTRAST_PAIRS` stay at or above 4.5:1.
21. Touch targets on coarse pointers stay at least 44px.
22. A slow page is not premium. Public routes share one editorial chunk. Do not add a font or image without a reason.

## Completion

23. A surface is done when its route, evidence, empty or failure state, and test or structural check exist.
24. Specifying a surface in this constitution does not mark it implemented. Status lives in `SHERIVS_EXPERIENCE_STATUS.md`.
