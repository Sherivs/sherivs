## Status method

Each capability is assessed separately for **designed**, **implemented**, **tested**, **deployed**, and **production-verified**. `Yes` means the cited evidence supports that stage; `Partial` means only a subset or an unwired contract is evidenced; `No` means no implementation/evidence for that stage was found; `Unknown` means the inspected records do not establish it; `Conflicted` means repository records disagree. A design document or unit test is not evidence of deployment. “Production-verified” reflects repository-recorded live evidence only, not a new live-system check by this report.

The primary capability inventory is the 33-area list in `architecture/coregrid-mvp-architecture/foundation/COREGRID_V1_SCOPE_LOCK.json`. The older 41-row matrix in `architecture/campaigns/V1-PROGRAM/V1-CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md` is retained as historical input, not copied as current status: its old counts and several status claims conflict with later program records and the V5.3 scope catalog. The scope lock and V1 catalog identify the authoritative V1 baseline; the newer owner directive adds the bounded epics in the companion scope lock.
