## Identifier and capability rules

- The full existing `platform_roles` catalogue is `platform_admin`, `support`, `engineering`, `security`, `finance`, `sales`, and `operations`; effective capabilities are the union of all assigned platform-role capability arrays (`frontend/src/lib/platform-staff/catalogue.ts:7-16,21-66`).
- Console presentation labels are not database role identifiers. `presentationRoleFor()` chooses one label in priority order, while `viewerCapabilities()` unions capabilities; multiple role assignments are not reduced to the displayed label (`frontend/src/lib/internal-console/roles.ts:34-53,363-380`).
- `platform_owner` is synthetic. The role-workspace mapper inserts it for a super-admin; it does not insert a `platform_roles` record (`frontend/src/lib/internal-console/role-workspace.ts:22-30,95-105`; `database/supabase/migrations/20261027002000_sherivs_031_control_plane_engines.sql:82-90,180-199`).
- The database is the authority for console capability checks. The TypeScript catalogue is a UI mirror, not a second policy engine (`frontend/src/lib/platform-staff/catalogue.ts:1-6,61-66`; `database/supabase/migrations/20261020000150_sherivs_008_platform_staff.sql:74-83`).

## Existing roles outside the six-workspace envelope

Security, Finance, and Sales remain compatibility role codes and retain their capabilities, but no longer provision dedicated workspaces. Security resolves to Platform Administration; Finance and Sales resolve to Operations. This is a workspace mapping only: it does not add capabilities or create another role engine. The six workspace definitions are Platform Operations, Platform Administration, Operations, Engineering, Support, and Personal Workspace.

## Boundary with #176 / draft PR #196

M009-A1's `RoleTemplate`, `RoleAssignment`, `Responsibility`, `Permission`, and `WorkspaceManifest` are for a tenant/institution role engine, not platform workforce identity (`#176`, merged PR #196, `frontend/src/lib/workspaces/contract.ts`). Its SQL requires active tenant membership and active `public.users` identity for assignments and effective permissions (`database/supabase/migrations/20261101000010_sherivs_m009_a1_role_responsibility_engine.sql:45-61,460-487`). Do not map `platform_staff_roles` or super-admin status into a Scholar tenant assignment.
